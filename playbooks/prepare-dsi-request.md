# Playbook — prepare a DSI request

Generate a one-page request with these headings.

## Objet
What must be enabled or approved.

## Cas d'usage
Who uses it, how often, and what business problem it solves.

## Architecture
- Microsoft product(s);
- harness/runtime;
- trigger;
- identity;
- data sources;
- tools/connectors;
- target channel.

## Licences et coût
- user licenses assumed;
- Copilot Credits exposure;
- Power Platform/Azure exposure;
- proposed pilot spending limit;
- live sources and verification date.

## Sécurité
- Entra authentication;
- least privilege;
- DLP/connectors;
- SharePoint/Purview considerations;
- human approval for sensitive actions.

## Administration demandée
Exact toggles, policies, environments, groups, approvals or billing policies required.

## Pilote
Population, duration, success criteria, usage telemetry, cost ceiling.

## Rollback
How to disable access, unpublish the agent, unlink billing, or stop the flow.

## Validation
Business owner, technical owner, DSI/security/DPO checkpoints as applicable.
